# Introduction
Domain Service is a business logic that does not naturally belong to only one entity ([[Software Engineering - Architecture concepts - Entity|link]]) but is related to multiple entities. 

For example:
- Money transfer between bank accounts - related to account and transaction entities
- Pricing calculation - related to order, customer, product and discount entities
- Recommendation scoring - related to user, product, interact history entities
