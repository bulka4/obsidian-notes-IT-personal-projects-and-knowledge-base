# Introduction
Latency percentiles describe the distribution of response times, showing how fast requests are for different portions of users.

The idea:
> "Instead of looking only at the average latency, how slow are the worst experiences?"

Common percentiles:
- **P50 (median)** — 50% of requests are faster, 50% are slower.
- **P95** — 95% of requests are faster, 5% are slower.
- **P99** — 99% of requests are faster, 1% are slower.
